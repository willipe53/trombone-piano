## ![Keyboard](piano) Keyboard

Polyphonic. Slide the keyboard back and forth with the mahogany strip. On a phone this screen stays landscape. The opening screen, help, and the games turn with the device. On an iPad this screen turns too.

## ![Key size](straighten) Key size

Sets how large the keys are.

## ![Show notes](toggle_on) Show notes

Prints the note name on each white key. The circle next to the switch opens a palette. The chosen color is stored as an opaque hex.

## ![Show key](toggle_on) Show key

Marks keys outside the key signature. The hatch circle sets the pattern. Tapping it opens the five hatches.

## ![Keep alive](toggle_on) Keep alive

Leaves the screen on while the app is open. The switch is on the phone and tablet. The web does not show it.

## ![Trombone](music_note) Trombone

On plays the trombone and shows the horn. The slide moves to the position of the last note played. Off plays the piano. Tap the horn to switch between the straight and trigger drawings. The trigger horn shows B1 through Eb2 with the trigger thrown and prints T positions.

## ![Show positions](toggle_on) Show positions

Prints the slide position on keys the horn can play and over notes on the staff. The circle next to the switch opens a palette for that color.

## ![MIDI](toggle_on) MIDI

The MIDI switch is on iPhone and iPad. Android and the web do not show it. On sends the keys to a computer and stays quiet on this device. Off plays here. The button beside the switch opens Bluetooth. The mark is gray while MIDI is off, white while MIDI is on, blinks while advertising, and turns blue when a computer connects. See below for MIDI details.

## ![Key signature](signature) Key signature

Sets the signature on the score and the SHOW KEY marks.

## ![Score](library_music) Score

Notes you play are written on the right. Trombone uses tenor and bass clef. Piano uses treble and bass. CLEAR SCORE empties it. With show positions on, the trombone score also prints the position above each note. Pinch the staff to grow or shrink it, and slide it to look around. RECENTER puts that view back.

## ![Golf](golf_course) Golf

Golf, bass and Golf, tenor deal the same game in that clef. Columns of face-up notes, and one note in the waste with its slide position showing. Take the front note of a column when a position of that note is one stop from the waste. The short 2nd sits with 2, so it is next to 1 and 3. The position prints on the note as it lands. A take that is not a neighbor does not move, and the position stays hidden until a legal take or a peek. Undo lifts the last take. Clearing the columns finishes the hole. Easier removes ledger lines or shortens the deal. Harder adds ledger lines or columns. Next deals another hole in the same clef. With sound on, a legal take plays that note, a peek plays the note you looked up, and clearing the columns plays the hole.

## ![Scale inventory](queue_music) Scale inventory

One octave of a named major key on the bass staff, with notes missing. Place the missing notes. A wrong note does not land. Clearing the octave finishes the round. Easier removes a blank, an accidental, or the ledger lines. Harder adds a blank, then accidentals, then ledger lines. Next deals another key of the same size. With sound on, a placed note plays, and clearing the octave plays the scale.

## ![MIDI details](info) MIDI details

With MIDI on, the keys send note on and note off, and this device stays quiet. The staff, the horn, and the slide positions still follow the keys. The computer plays its own sound. This section applies on iPhone and iPad.

GarageBand on a Mac. Use a data cable and unlock the device. Best MIDI connectivity comes from connecting the iOS device directly to the computer, not through a third-party USB hub. Turn MIDI on. On the Mac, open Audio MIDI Setup, choose Window, Show MIDI Studio, and enable the device if it is gray. That is once per device. The device icon is the input. MIDI Studio does not show a port named Trombone Piano.

In GarageBand, add a Software Instrument track. The instrument does not matter. Select the track, turn its record button on, and play. GarageBand Settings, Audio/MIDI, leaves MIDI Controller on None. That menu is for control surfaces. An entry under Input Device is the microphone. MIDI Status flashes when a key arrives. If nothing flashes, quit GarageBand, rescan MIDI, and open GarageBand again.

Logic, Ableton, and MuseScore on the Mac use that same device. Other apps on this device can use the name Trombone Piano.

Windows, Wi-Fi. Put the device and the PC on the same network. A guest network that isolates devices will not work. Install the free rtpMIDI driver, add a session, enable it, and connect to the device. If the device is not in the list, add it by its address, and allow that UDP port and the next one through the firewall. In MuseScore, select that session as the MIDI input, start note input, and play. The USB cable does not make the device a MIDI device on Windows.

Bluetooth. Turn MIDI on, tap the button beside the switch, and turn advertising on. The advertise switch is saved. The mark blinks while this device is advertising and turns blue when a computer connects. On a Mac, pair from the Bluetooth control in MIDI Studio. On Windows 10 or later, pair it as a Bluetooth MIDI device, then choose Trombone Piano as the MIDI input.

## ![Privacy](privacy_tip) Privacy

Notes and settings stay on the device. Nothing is sent to the developer. While MIDI is on, any computer on the local network can connect and receive the notes, and a cable-connected computer or another app on this device can receive them too. Bluetooth sends notes only after advertising is turned on. Privacy policy: [https://trombonepiano.com/privacy-policy.html](https://trombonepiano.com/privacy-policy.html)
