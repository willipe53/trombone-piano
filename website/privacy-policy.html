Privacy policy

Trombone Piano collects nothing. Notes and settings stay on the device. Nothing is sent to Extent Devices.

While MIDI is on, any computer on the local network can connect and receive the notes. A cable-connected computer or another app on this device can receive them too. Bluetooth sends notes only after advertising is turned on.
