Support

Trombone Piano is a touch keyboard for iPhone and iPad. It plays trombone or piano on the device, shows slide positions, writes the notes on a staff, and can send MIDI.

Trombone Piano is an open source app. Feel free to contribute and comment. The repository is github.com/willipe53/trombone-piano.
